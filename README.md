# Frontier EdTech

**Raising thinkers, makers, and explorers.**

Frontier EdTech is an education research and innovation initiative exploring how to reinvent K–12 education for children aged 6–17 in the age of AI.

> The world has changed. Has education?

## The idea

Education has long rewarded remembering answers. In a world where AI can generate them, that is no longer enough. We want to give more room to the questions that matter: Can children think for themselves? Spot a problem nobody has pointed out? Challenge assumptions and turn ideas into reality?

## Four capabilities

1. **Critical Thinkers**: question thoughtfully, think independently.
2. **Curious Learners**: stay curious, keep discovering.
3. **Creative Builders**: imagine possibilities, make things happen.
4. **Fearless Adventurers**: embrace the unknown, find your own way.

## Philosophy

**Learn deeply. Explore freely. Create boldly.**
Not learning instead of doing, but learning through thinking, making, and doing.

## Early experiments

- [Frontier Kids Club](https://www.frontierkidsclub.com/): a pilot exploring curiosity, creativity, and hands-on discovery.
- [Day of AI Singapore](https://www.dayofaisingapore.com/): AI education and exploration for Singapore's learning community.
